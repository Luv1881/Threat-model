= Assignment by Function
:fn-risk-findings: footnote:riskfinding[Risk finding paragraphs are clickable and link to the corresponding chapter.]

This chapter clusters and assigns the risks by functions which are most likely able to check and mitigate them: In total *12 potential risks* have been identified during the threat modeling process of which *0 should be checked by Business Side*, *8 should be checked by Architecture*, *0 should be checked by Development*, and *4 should be checked by Operations*.{fn-risk-findings}

== Business Side

== Architecture
<<undocumented-entry-point,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Stage III - Undocumented Application Entry Point*: 5/5 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _Medium_ impact.#>>::
Undocumented entry points are excluded from threat analysis, leaving unknown attack surface that attackers can exploit without detection or mitigation.


<<weak-auth-on-entry-point,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Stage VI - Weak Authentication Strength on Internet-Exposed Entry Point*: 1/1 unmitigated Risk - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Full account takeover, lateral movement into internal systems, and data exfiltration. Credential stuffing attacks against password-only endpoints succeed at statistically predictable rates given leaked credential databases.


<<internet-reachable-datastore,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Stage VII - Internet-Reachable Datastore With No Mitigation*: 2/2 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _Very High_ impact.#>>::
Mass data exfiltration of all stored records, ransomware encryption of datastores, complete loss of data integrity, and regulatory breach notification obligations.



== Development

== Operations
<<per-operation-rate-limiting-missing,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Stage V - Custom-Developed Service Without Per-Operation Rate Limiting*: 2/2 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _Medium_ impact.#>>::
Attackers can enumerate users, brute-force credentials, bulk-download sensitive data, or exhaust backend resources by sending unlimited requests to unthrottled operations. Business process integrity degrades when operations can be repeated without throttling.


<<no-rate-limiting-public-api,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Stage V - No Rate Limiting on Internet-Exposed API*: 1/1 unmitigated Risk - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Brute-force attacks against authentication endpoints, mass data harvesting via enumeration, DDoS amplification, and billing abuse on pay-per-use endpoints.


<<missing-threat-scenario,[.LowRisk]#Low: *Stage I - No Threat Scenarios Defined for Internet-Exposed Entry Points*: 1/1 unmitigated Risk - Exploitation likelihood is _Unlikely_ with _Low_ impact.#>>::
Without explicit threat actor profiles and attack scenarios, mitigations are chosen reactively rather than proactively, leaving systematic threat actor capabilities unaddressed.
