= Assignment by Function
:fn-risk-findings: footnote:riskfinding[Risk finding paragraphs are clickable and link to the corresponding chapter.]

This chapter clusters and assigns the risks by functions which are most likely able to check and mitigate them: In total *89 potential risks* have been identified during the threat modeling process of which *7 should be checked by Business Side*, *35 should be checked by Architecture*, *13 should be checked by Development*, and *34 should be checked by Operations*.{fn-risk-findings}

== Business Side
<<missing-authentication-second-factor,[.MediumRisk]#Medium: *Missing Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)*: 1/1 unmitigated Risk - Exploitation likelihood is _Unlikely_ with _Medium_ impact.#>>::
If this risk is unmitigated, attackers might be able to access or modify highly sensitive data without strong authentication.

<<trike-residual-risk-unaccepted,[.MediumRisk]#Medium: *Trike - Information Asset Container With High Sensitivity Without Formal Residual Risk Acceptance*: 6/6 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Unlikely_ with _Medium_ impact.#>>::
Unaccepted residual risks indicate incomplete risk treatment. Management and the risk committee may not be aware of ongoing exposure. Regulators may view the absence of formal risk acceptance as evidence of inadequate risk governance, leading to findings during audits and increased liability in the event of a breach.

<<missing-authentication,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Missing Authentication*: 2/2 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::

<<trike-actor-no-trust-level,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Trike - Actor Without Defined Trust Level*: 1/1 unmitigated Risk - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _Medium_ impact.#>>::
Without a defined trust level, the actor cannot be properly placed in the Trike action matrix. Rights may be over-granted by default, enabling unauthorised read, write, or delete operations on sensitive assets. Unclassified actors are a common source of privilege escalation and access control bypass findings.


<<trike-asset-no-action-matrix,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Trike - High-Confidentiality Datastore Without Action Matrix Review*: 7/7 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Unreviewed action matrices on high-confidentiality datastores mean that access rights may be broader than intended. Any actor — including low-trust or untrusted actors — may have implicit read or write access that was never explicitly denied. This is the primary mechanism for privilege escalation in Trike-modelled systems.


<<trike-matrix-unreviewed,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Trike - High-Confidentiality Datastore Without Reviewed Action Matrix*: 7/7 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Without a reviewed action matrix, there is no documented control boundary for the datastore. Actors — including low-trust or untrusted ones — may have implicit access that was never explicitly denied. Gaps between intended and implemented access are the primary source of data exfiltration and privilege escalation in Trike-modelled systems.


<<trike-high-trust-unmonitored,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Trike - High-Trust Actor Without Audit Logging*: 1/1 unmitigated Risk - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
High-trust actors acting without audit trails enable insider threat, privilege abuse, and make forensic investigation impossible after security incidents. Regulatory frameworks (SOC 2, PCI-DSS, HIPAA) require demonstrable audit trails for privileged operations on systems processing sensitive data. Absence of logging constitutes a compliance failure in most regulated environments.
