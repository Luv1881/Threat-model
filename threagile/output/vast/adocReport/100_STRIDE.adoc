= STRIDE Classification of Identified Risks

This chapter clusters and classifies the risks by STRIDE categories: In total *10 potential risks* have been identified during the threat modeling process of which *0 in the Spoofing* category, *0 in the Tampering* category, *2 in the Repudiation* category, *2 in the Information Disclosure* category, *3 in the Denial of Service* category, and *3 in the Elevation of Privilege* category.{fn-risk-findings}

No risk identified.

== Tampering
No risk identified.

== Repudiation
<<operational-unmonitored-critical-process,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Operational Threat - Critical Business Process Without Operational Monitoring*: 2/2 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Undetected breaches, inability to investigate incidents, compliance failures, and violation of SLA accountability requirements.



== Information Disclosure
<<application-secrets-across-processes,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Application Threat - Strictly-Confidential Asset Bridges Business Process Contexts*: 2/2 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Credential exposure in any one process context compromises all other processes the asset supports, amplifying the breach blast radius.



== Denial of Service
<<operational-critical-process-no-redundancy,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Operational Threat - Critical Business Process Supported by Non-Redundant Assets*: 3/3 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Complete outage of a critical business process due to failure or targeted attack against the sole supporting technical asset. No failover path means maximum downtime.



== Elevation of Privilege
<<operational-shared-runtime-critical-process,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Operational Threat - Critical Business Process on Shared Runtime*: 3/3 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Lateral movement from a compromised co-located workload to the infrastructure supporting a critical business process, causing service outage, data exfiltration, or integrity violations at the process level.
