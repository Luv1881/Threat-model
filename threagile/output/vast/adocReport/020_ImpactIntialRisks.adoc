= Impact Analysis Of 11 Initial Risks In 4 Categories
:fn-risk-findings: footnote:riskfinding[Risk finding paragraphs are clickable and link to the corresponding chapter.]
The most prevalent impacts of the *11 initial risks* (distributed over *4 risk categories*) are (taking the severity ratings into account and using the highest for each category)!{fn-risk-findings}

<<application-secrets-across-processes,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Application Threat - Strictly-Confidential Asset Bridges Business Process Contexts*: 2 Initial Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Credential exposure in any one process context compromises all other processes the asset supports, amplifying the breach blast radius.


<<operational-critical-process-no-redundancy,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Operational Threat - Critical Business Process Supported by Non-Redundant Assets*: 3 Initial Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Complete outage of a critical business process due to failure or targeted attack against the sole supporting technical asset. No failover path means maximum downtime.


<<operational-unmonitored-critical-process,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Operational Threat - Critical Business Process Without Operational Monitoring*: 3 Initial Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Undetected breaches, inability to investigate incidents, compliance failures, and violation of SLA accountability requirements.


<<operational-shared-runtime-critical-process,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Operational Threat - Critical Business Process on Shared Runtime*: 3 Initial Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Lateral movement from a compromised co-located workload to the infrastructure supporting a critical business process, causing service outage, data exfiltration, or integrity violations at the process level.
