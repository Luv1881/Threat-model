= Assignment by Function
:fn-risk-findings: footnote:riskfinding[Risk finding paragraphs are clickable and link to the corresponding chapter.]

This chapter clusters and assigns the risks by functions which are most likely able to check and mitigate them: In total *35 potential risks* have been identified during the threat modeling process of which *1 should be checked by Business Side*, *7 should be checked by Architecture*, *4 should be checked by Development*, and *23 should be checked by Operations*.{fn-risk-findings}

== Business Side

If this risk is unmitigated, attackers might be able to access or modify highly sensitive data without strong authentication.

<<missing-csp-header,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Missing Content-Security-Policy Header on Web Entry Point*: 1/1 unmitigated Risk - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _Medium_ impact.#>>::
