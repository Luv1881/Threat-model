== Repudiation
<<detecting-no-audit-logging,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Detectability - PII Flows Without Audit Logging*: 6/6 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _Medium_ impact.#>>::
Inability to detect unauthorized access to personal data, failure to meet GDPR accountability obligations, and potential inability to meet breach notification deadlines.



== Information Disclosure
<<data-disclosure-pii-unencrypted-link,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Data Disclosure - Personal Data Transmitted Over Unencrypted Channel*: 2/2 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Exposure of personal data to eavesdroppers, potential data breach notification obligations under GDPR Article 33, and significant regulatory penalties.

<<identifying-pii-stored-unencrypted,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Identifiability - Personal Data Stored Without Encryption at Rest*: 6/6 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _High_ impact.#>>::
Physical or logical access to unencrypted volumes directly exposes personal data, enabling identification of natural persons and constituting a reportable data breach.


<<pii-client-side-storage,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Non-Repudiation - PII or Auth Tokens Accessible in Browser-Side Storage*: 1/1 unmitigated Risk - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _Medium_ impact.#>>::
Stolen session tokens allow account takeover and impersonation. The original user cannot repudiate actions performed by the attacker. Stolen PII violates GDPR data minimisation and storage limitation principles.


<<unawareness-no-consent-technology,[.ElevatedRisk]#Elevated: *Unawareness - PII Processed Without a Consent Management Component*: 1/1 unmitigated Risk - Exploitation likelihood is _Likely_ with _Medium_ impact.#>>::
Data subjects cannot exercise GDPR rights. Regulatory investigation risk and class-action exposure in jurisdictions with private right of action.


<<linking-pii-multi-boundary,[.MediumRisk]#Medium: *Linkability - Same PII Data Asset Processed Across Multiple Trust Boundaries*: 12/12 unmitigated Risks - Exploitation likelihood is _Unlikely_ with _Medium_ impact.#>>::
Aggregation of personal data enables profiling, re-identification, and violations of GDPR data minimisation and purpose limitation principles (Articles 5(1)(b) and 5(1)(c)).
